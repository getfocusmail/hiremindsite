Interview practice app FAQ

Common questions about using HireMind to prepare for job interviews.

What is HireMind?

HireMind is an AI interview practice app for iOS. It helps you rehearse with live voice mock interviews, role-specific question libraries, and coaching feedback—so you build confidence before the real thing.

Is HireMind good for practicing job interviews?

Yes. HireMind mirrors real interview flow with follow-up prompts, scores answers on clarity and relevance, and gives coaching notes you can use to improve your next response.

Does HireMind include mock interview questions?

Absolutely. You get quick practice reps, a daily interview question, and searchable libraries for software, healthcare, sales, finance, skilled trades, and service roles.

Is HireMind free?

HireMind has a free plan with daily questions, streak tracking, role libraries, and unlimited standard practice. Pro adds unlimited live AI voice interviews and deeper AI grading.

What devices does HireMind support?

HireMind runs on iPhone and iPad. Download it from the App Store to practice interviews by voice or text whenever you have a few minutes.
